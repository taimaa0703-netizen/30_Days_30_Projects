CLASSVERSE

Turn a lesson into a shared classroom adventure. Teachers host live missions; students join from their own devices, answer challenges, earn points, and shape the story together.

  • English, Hebrew, and Arabic with right-to-left layouts
  • Live room codes, quizzes, student-created questions, and story votes
  • Designed for phones and classroom screens

The live classroom runs on a separate Node.js service. Deploy it once with the button below; after it is ready, use the live classroom link.
